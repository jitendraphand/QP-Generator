How to use this site

  1. Know the exam — read the About NMTC page to understand the levels, stages and question style.
  2. Map your topics — check the Syllabus for your class level and note weak areas.
  3. Follow the plan — use the Study Planner to structure 8 weeks of preparation.
  4. Practise daily — solve problems on the Practice page and read every solution, even when you get the answer right.
  5. Simulate the exam — take a Mock Test each week and revise from the Formula Sheets.
⚠️ This is an independent, community-made study site and is not affiliated with the AMTI. Always confirm registration dates, fees and the exact exam pattern on the official AMTI website: amtionline.com.
