About the NMTC

The National Mathematics Talent Contest is India's long-running school mathematics talent examination, conducted annually by the Association of Mathematics Teachers of India (AMTI).

What makes NMTC different?

The NMTC is not a test of speed or syllabus recall. Its stated goal is to spot mathematical talent — the ability to reason, spot patterns and construct arguments. Questions are typically puzzle-like and need ingenuity rather than memorised procedures. That's why preparing for NMTC also builds a strong base for RMO/INMO and other olympiads.

Contest levels

Level Contest name Classes
Primary Gauss Contest Std V & VI
Sub-Junior Kaprekar Contest Std VII & VIII
Junior Bhaskara Contest Std IX & X
Inter Ramanujan Contest Std XI & XII
Senior Aryabhata Contest Undergraduate students

The two stages

Stage 1 — Preliminary Screening

  • Held at the student's own school or a nearby centre, usually around August–September.
  • Objective / short-answer format (multiple choice and fill-in answers).
  • Duration is typically about 2 hours.
  • Tests speed and insight — clever shortcuts matter.
  • Top performers (roughly the best ~10%) qualify for the Final.

Stage 2 — Final Descriptive

  • Usually held around October–November at designated centres.
  • Fully descriptive: you must write complete solutions with justification — answers alone earn little credit.
  • Duration is typically about 3 hours for 8–10 problems.
  • Prizes, medals and certificates are awarded to the top rankers at each level.

Eligibility & registration

  • Open to school students of the respective classes across India (and to undergraduate students for the Aryabhata contest).
  • Registration normally happens through your school. Schools enrol as centres with the AMTI; ask your mathematics teacher first.
  • Individual registration options and the exact fee are announced each year by the AMTI.
  • The medium of the paper is English (some regional-language support has been offered in various years).

Why participate?

🏅 Recognition

Merit certificates and prizes at the national level look great in a student's profile and build real confidence.

🧠 Deeper thinking

NMTC-style problems train genuine problem solving, not formula plugging — skills that carry into every future exam.

🚀 Olympiad pathway

The problem style overlaps strongly with PRMO/RMO-style olympiad mathematics, making NMTC an ideal first step into the olympiad world.

⚠️ Dates, fees, format and the number of questions can change from year to year. Treat the details above as a general guide and confirm the current year's notification on amtionline.com.
