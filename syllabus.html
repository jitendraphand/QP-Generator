Syllabus & Topics

The AMTI does not prescribe a rigid chapter list — problems are drawn from the mathematics a student of that class can reasonably know, with emphasis on ingenuity. The topic map below is compiled from the style of past NMTC papers.

🎯 Primary — Gauss Contest Std V–VI

  • Arithmetic: the four operations, fractions, decimals, unitary method, simple percentages, averages.
  • Number sense: place value, divisibility rules, factors and multiples, HCF/LCM, odd–even reasoning.
  • Patterns & sequences: number patterns, shape patterns, magic squares.
  • Geometry & measurement: perimeter and area of rectangles/squares, angles, clocks and calendars.
  • Logic puzzles: substitution puzzles, cryptarithms with pictures/symbols, counting figures.

🎯 Sub-Junior — Kaprekar Contest Std VII–VIII

  • Number theory: divisibility, remainders, digits of powers, primes and composites, HCF/LCM problems.
  • Algebra: linear equations, simple identities such as (a±b)², exponents, ratio and proportion, percentages.
  • Geometry: angle chasing, triangle properties, polygon angle sums, congruence basics, areas of triangles and quadrilaterals.
  • Counting: systematic listing, simple combinatorics (handshakes, diagonals), pigeonhole-flavoured puzzles.
  • Word problems: ages, speed–time–distance, work, mixtures.

🎯 Junior — Bhaskara Contest Std IX–X

  • Algebra: polynomial identities and factorisation, quadratic equations and the symmetric functions of roots, inequalities (AM–GM basics), sequences and series.
  • Number theory: modular arithmetic, last digits, divisor counting, Diophantine equations, properties of primes.
  • Geometry: circles (chords, tangents, cyclic quadrilaterals), similar triangles, Pythagoras, areas, incircle/circumcircle basics.
  • Combinatorics: permutations & combinations, counting with cases, pigeonhole principle, colouring/invariance ideas.
  • Miscellaneous: functional-style puzzles, telescoping sums, floor/ceiling basics.

🎯 Inter — Ramanujan Contest Std XI–XII

  • Algebra: polynomials and their roots, complex numbers, inequalities (AM–GM, Cauchy–Schwarz), recurrence relations, mathematical induction.
  • Number theory: congruences, Fermat's little theorem, GCD arguments, Diophantine equations.
  • Combinatorics: binomial theorem and identities, advanced counting, inclusion–exclusion, elementary probability.
  • Geometry & trigonometry: advanced Euclidean geometry, trigonometric identities and equations, coordinate geometry.
  • Analysis flavour: sequences and limits, functional equations, floor function problems.

How the two stages differ in demand

Skill Stage 1 (Preliminary) Stage 2 (Final)
Format Objective / short answer Full written solutions
Key skill Fast, accurate insight; elimination tricks Rigorous proof writing and clear presentation
Practice with Practice questions and timed mocks Past final papers — write every solution out in full
💡 Tip: NMTC rewards depth over coverage. It is far better to master reasoning in a few core areas (numbers, angles, counting) than to skim everything. When you practise, always ask why the trick works.
